🌊 What is a Sea Pool?

A sea pool is a blue space located in the intertidal zone that provides safer swimming conditions than the open ocean, thanks to integrated walls, breakwaters, or rock barriers offering protection against waves, marine fauna, and strong currents.

📌 How to add a Sea Pool:

Navigate to your known sea pool on the map, click on its location to set a pin, then press the "+ Add a Sea Pool" button and complete the form.
